equivalents:
  - "anything / everything / whatever (it may be) — question word 何 + でも, sweeping in every option without exception"
nuance: "何 ('what') + でも turns into 'no matter what → anything at all'. It's the affirmative free-choice indefinite: 何{なに}でも食{た}べる means 'eats anything (you name it)'. The same 〜でも pattern builds the whole family — 誰{だれ}でも (anyone), どこでも (anywhere), いつでも (anytime). Don't confuse with 何{なに}も + negative, which means 'nothing'."
