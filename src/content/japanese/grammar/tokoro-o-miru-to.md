equivalents:
  - "judging from the fact that ~ / seeing that ~ — takes a scene the speaker is observing as the basis for a guess about what lies behind it"
nuance: "You see someone in a certain state (ところ = the scene/situation), and 〜を見{み}ると ('seeing that') turns it into evidence for an inference. 黙{だま}っているところを見{み}ると、怒{おこ}っているのだろう ('judging from how he's gone quiet, he must be angry'). The conclusion is a guess, so the second clause almost always ends in a conjecture form (らしい・ようだ・のだろう・に違{ちが}いない). It's about reading a present, observable situation — not citing an abstract fact."
keySentence:
  jp: "電気{でんき}が消{き}えているところを見{み}ると、もう寝{ね}たらしい。"
  en: "Judging from the fact that the lights are off, it seems she's already gone to bed."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Verb (plain, often ている/た), adjective, N + である — describing the observed state"
    form: "V/A(plain) + ところを見ると"
    example: "笑{わら}っている → 笑{わら}っているところを見ると"
usageSetting: "The speaker reasons aloud from something visible in front of them; the main clause is a tentative conclusion, not a fact."
examples:
  - jp: "あんなに食{た}べているところを見{み}ると、よほどお腹{なか}が空{す}いていたのだろう。"
    en: "Seeing how much he's eating, he must have been really hungry."
  - jp: "返事{へんじ}がないところを見{み}ると、彼{かれ}はもう出{で}かけたのかもしれない。"
    en: "Judging from the lack of a reply, he may have already left."
  - jp: "顔色{かおいろ}が悪{わる}いところを見{み}ると、体調{たいちょう}がよくないようだ。"
    en: "From the look of how pale she is, she seems to be unwell."
contrasts:
  - slug: "koto-kara"
    label: "ことから"
    distinction: "ことから cites an established *fact* as grounds and can lead to a firm conclusion or an explanation of origins; ところを見ると reads a *scene the speaker is watching* and lands on a tentative guess (らしい・だろう)."
  - slug: "tokoro-kara"
    label: "ところから"
    distinction: "ところから presents a source/circumstance that something stems from (often how a name or situation arose); ところを見ると is specifically 'I observe this, therefore I infer ~'."
  - slug: "rashii"
    label: "らしい"
    distinction: "らしい / ようだ are the conjecture endings ところを見ると naturally pairs with — ところを見ると supplies the visible *evidence*, and the らしい states the inferred conclusion."
notes:
