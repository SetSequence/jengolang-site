    keySentence:

    examples:

        en: "It's already April, but it's cold like winter."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "noun"
    form: "N + みたい (no の)"
    example: "犬{いぬ}みたい / 天使{てんし}みたい"
  - attaches_to: "plain verb / i-adjective"
    form: "V/A(plain) + みたい"
    example: "降{ふ}ったみたい / 高{たか}いみたい"
  - attaches_to: "na-adjective"
    form: "na-adj (drop だ) + みたい"

restrictions:
  - text: "Unlike ようだ, みたい attaches **directly** to a noun or na-adjective — no の, no な. 天使{てんし}みたい, never ✗天使{てんし}のみたい; 静{しず}かみたい, never ✗静{しず}かなみたい."
variants:
  - form: "みたいです / みたいに・みたいな"
    note: "In polite speech the copula surfaces as みたいです; the adverbial/adnominal forms are みたいに・みたいな (see 〜みたいに・〜みたいな)."
contrasts:
  - slug: "youda"
    label: "ようだ"
    distinction: "みたい is the casual spoken form of ようだ for both senses (雨{あめ}が降{ふ}ったみたい = 降{ふ}ったようだ). ようだ is neutral-to-written; みたい sounds conversational."
  - slug: "souda"
    label: "そうだ (hearsay)"
    distinction: "みたい = the speaker's own inference from what they observe; 伝聞{でんぶん}の そうだ = secondhand report ('I hear ~'). 降{ふ}ったみたい = it looks like it rained; 降{ふ}ったそうだ = I hear it rained."
    sense: "conjecture from evidence"
  - slug: "rashii"
    label: "らしい"
    distinction: "らしい leans on hearsay or what's typical and feels less personal; みたい is the speaker's direct impression."
    sense: "conjecture from evidence"
  - slug: "ppoi"
    label: "〜っぽい"
    distinction: "〜っぽい = has a ~ tendency / -ish (子供{こども}っぽい = childish, often a flaw); みたい = resembles / seems like (子供{こども}みたい = like a child). Tendency vs resemblance."
    sense: "resemblance"
