equivalents:
  - "~, and what's more ~ / ~, plus ~ — stacks reasons or qualities, presenting them as cumulative grounds (often for an implied conclusion)"
nuance: "し joins clauses while signalling 'and on top of that' — it piles up reasons or features that all point the same way. Often it leads to a stated or unstated conclusion: 安{やす}いし、近{ちか}いし(、ここにしよう) ('it's cheap, and it's close, [so let's pick here]'). A single trailing し can hint at an unspoken reason: 疲{つか}れたし… ('I'm tired, and…'). Unlike から, it presents reasons as a loose accumulation rather than one direct cause."
keySentence:
  jp: "この店{みせ}は安{やす}いし、おいしい。"
  en: "This place is cheap, and what's more, it's tasty."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Plain-form clauses (and です/ます in polite speech)"
    form: "… plain + し、…"
    example: "近{ちか}いし / 静{しず}かだし / 学生{がくせい}だし"
examples:
  - jp: "今日{きょう}は寒{さむ}いし、家{いえ}にいよう。"
    en: "It's cold today, so (for that and other reasons) let's stay home."
  - jp: "彼{かれ}は優{やさ}しいし、頼{たよ}りになる。"
    en: "He's kind, and on top of that, dependable."
  - jp: "もう遅{おそ}いし、そろそろ帰{かえ}ります。"
    en: "It's late, and (anyway) I'll be heading home soon."
contrasts:
  - slug: "kara"
    label: "から (reason)"
    distinction: "から gives one direct cause ('because X, Y'); し offers reasons as a loose, expandable pile ('X, and what's more, …'), often softer and implying there's more behind the decision."
  - slug: "shi-shi"
    label: "〜し〜し"
    distinction: "Repeating し (〜し〜し) makes the cumulative listing explicit, spelling out two or more grounds side by side."
notes:
  - text: "A clause ending in a lone し often deliberately trails off, leaving the conclusion (or a further reason) unsaid: 高{たか}いし…（やめておく）."
