equivalents:
  - "as for ~ / (in contrast) ~ — beyond marking the topic, は singles one thing out against an implied alternative, or limits the scope of what is asserted"
nuance: "On top of its basic topic role, は carries an implicit *contrast*: saying X は flags that something else may differ. ビールは飲{の}みます hints 'beer I'll drink (other drinks, maybe not)'. In negatives it narrows the scope of the denial — そんなに高{たか}くはない is 'not *that* expensive' (it is somewhat), softer than a flat 高{たか}くない. Placed after another particle (には, では, とは) it adds this same contrastive, limiting colour."
keySentence:
  jp: "お酒{さけ}は飲{の}みますが、たばこは吸{す}いません。"
  en: "I do drink alcohol, but I don't smoke."
usageSetting: "Stacks after other particles to add contrast (学校{がっこう}へは行{い}った, 友達{ともだち}とは話{はな}さない); replaces が/を when the noun is set up as a contrasted topic."
examples:

    en: "Today's no good, but tomorrow I can go. (today, as opposed to other days)"
  - jp: "一度{いちど}は富士山{ふじさん}に登{のぼ}ってみたい。"
    en: "At least once, I'd like to climb Mt. Fuji."
  - jp: "値段{ねだん}が高{たか}くはないが、安{やす}くもない。"
    en: "It's not exactly expensive, but it's not cheap either."
contrasts:
  - slug: "ga-2"
    label: "が"
    distinction: "が presents new information and the subject as fresh focus; は takes something as a known topic and often sets it against an alternative. 'AはB' frames A as the topic; 'AがB' answers 'which one?' with A."
  - slug: "mo"
    label: "も"
    distinction: "も adds 'too / also', including the item in a group; contrastive は does the opposite — it singles the item out and *excludes* others by implication."
notes:
  - text: "Pronounced わ as a particle though written は. The contrastive reading is context-driven: the same は is plain topic or pointed contrast depending on intonation and what alternative is implied."
