title: "の — the linking particle (A の B)"
canonical: "の"
reading: "の"
register: ["casual-spoken", "polite-spoken", "written-modern"]
keigo: "none"
freq: "essential"
jlpt: "N5"
family: "particle"
confidence: "high"
sources:
  volumes: "B"
noindex: false
equivalents:
  - "~'s / of ~ — links two nouns, with the first describing or owning the second (A の B = a B that is A's / related to A)"
keySentence:
  jp: "これは私{わたし}の本{ほん}です。"
  en: "This is my book."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun + Noun"
    form: "N1 + の + N2"
    example: "日本{にほん}の車{くるま} (a Japanese car)"
examples:
  - jp: "先生{せんせい}の話{はなし}は面白{おもしろ}い。"
    en: "The teacher's talk is interesting."
    note: "possession / belonging"
  - jp: "木{き}の机{つくえ}を買{か}った。"
    en: "I bought a wooden desk."
    note: "material / attribute"
  - jp: "友達{ともだち}の田中{たなか}さんに会{あ}った。"
    en: "I met my friend Tanaka."
    note: "apposition — の links two names for one person"
  - jp: "姉{あね}の友達{ともだち}の家{いえ}に泊{と}まった。"
    en: "I stayed at my sister's friend's house."
    note: "の chains stack"
restrictions:
  - text: "Do not use の to link a na-adjective to its noun — na-adjectives take な: 静{しず}かな部屋{へや} (a quiet room), never ×静{しず}かの部屋{へや}. の links noun-to-noun only."
notes:
  - text: "の can also stand in for a noun already understood from context: 赤{あか}いのが好{す}き = \"I like the red one.\""
