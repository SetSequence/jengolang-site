restrictions:

contrasts:
  - slug: "nikui"
    label: "〜にくい"
    distinction: "にくい is the everyday word for 'hard to ~,' covering physical and practical difficulty with almost any verb (歩{ある}きにくい, 飲{の}みにくい). 難{がた}い is literary, emotional/abstract, and limited to a small verb set (信{しん}じ難{がた}い, 忘{わす}れ難{がた}い)."
  - slug: "kaneru"
    label: "〜かねる"
    distinction: "Both express an inability to do something for non-physical reasons, but かねる is a polite, business-like way to decline on the speaker's own part (賛成{さんせい}しかねます = 'I'm afraid I cannot agree'). 難{がた}い describes the difficulty as an objective quality (賛成{さんせい}し難{がた}い = 'it is hard to agree')."
