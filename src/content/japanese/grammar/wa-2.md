keigo: "none"

jlpt: "N5"
family: "particle"
confidence: "high"
sources:
  volumes: "B"
noindex: false
equivalents:
  - "as for ~ / speaking of ~ — marks what the sentence is about (the topic), not a role like subject or object"
nuance: "は sets the topic — the thing already on the table — and says \"about this, here is the new information.\" Because it frames rather than identifies, it carries a contrastive undertone: 寿司{すし}は好{す}き often implies \"sushi (at least) I like,\" leaving other foods open. This framing role is why は and the subject-marker が feel interchangeable to beginners but are not."
keySentence:
  jp: "私{わたし}は学生{がくせい}です。"
  en: "I am a student."
usageSetting: "Written は, pronounced わ. Attaches to a noun or phrase; it replaces が or を, and stacks after other particles (には, では, とは)."
examples:
  - jp: "象{ぞう}は鼻{はな}が長{なが}い。"
    en: "As for elephants, their noses are long."
    note: "topic は + subject が in one sentence"
  - jp: "今日{きょう}は忙{いそが}しい。"
    en: "Today I'm busy."
  - jp: "コーヒーは飲{の}みますが、お酒{さけ}は飲{の}みません。"
    en: "I drink coffee, but I don't drink alcohol."
    note: "contrastive は"
  - jp: "日本{にほん}へは行{い}ったことがある。"
    en: "To Japan, I have been (at least)."
    note: "は stacked after へ"
restrictions:
  - text: "The answer to a question word takes が, not は: 「誰{だれ}が来{き}たの？」→「私{わたし}が来{き}た」, never 私{わたし}は来{き}た. New, asked-for information is marked by が."
contrasts:
  - slug: "ga-2"
    label: "が"
    distinction: "は marks the topic / known information (\"as for X\"); が marks the grammatical subject / new information (\"it is X that\"). は presents, が identifies. This is the single most common particle confusion."
notes:
  - text: "は as the topic particle is written with the hiragana は but always pronounced わ."
