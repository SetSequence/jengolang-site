nuance: "Repeating でも sets up two (or more) options and says the result holds no matter which: 雨{あめ}でも晴{は}れでも行{い}く ('I'll go whether it rains or shines'). It's the concessive 'even if' でも doubled into a 'be it A or B' frame, often built from opposites or a question word's poles. The predicate is the same for every listed case."
