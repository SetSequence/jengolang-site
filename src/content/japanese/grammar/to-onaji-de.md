equivalents:
  - "the same as ~ / just like ~ — states two things are identical, then continues the sentence (で = て-form of だ)"
nuance: "と同{おな}じ ('the same as ~') with で, the て-form of the copula, lets 'X is the same as Y' lead into a further clause: 去年{きょねん}と同{おな}じで、今年{ことし}も暑{あつ}い ('same as last year, this year is hot too'). The plain noun-modifying form is と同{おな}じ + noun (私{わたし}と同{おな}じ意見{いけん}, 'the same opinion as me'); と同{おな}じだ ends a sentence with it. と同{おな}じで means *identical in kind*, not merely close in degree."
keySentence:
  jp: "私{わたし}の答{こた}えは田中{たなか}さんと同{おな}じで、反対{はんたい}です。"
  en: "My answer is the same as Tanaka's — I'm against it."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun + と同じ (＋で / ＋だ / ＋N)"
    form: "N + と同{おな}じで / と同{おな}じN / と同{おな}じだ"
    example: "前{まえ}と同{おな}じで / これと同{おな}じ色{いろ}"
examples:

    en: "Same as last year, we're short-handed again this year."
  - jp: "値段{ねだん}は前{まえ}と同{おな}じで、量{りょう}が減{へ}った。"
    en: "The price is the same as before, but the amount went down."
  - jp: "考{かんが}え方{かた}があなたと同{おな}じで、安心{あんしん}した。"
    en: "Your way of thinking is the same as mine, which was a relief."
contrasts:
  - slug: "to-onaji-kurai"
    label: "〜と同じくらい"
    distinction: "と同{おな}じで = identical *in kind* ('the same as'); と同{おな}じくらい = equal *in degree* ('about as ~ as'). Sameness vs equal extent."
  - slug: "to-2"
    label: "と (with)"
    distinction: "The と here is the 'with/as' と that pairs the two compared items (Aと同{おな}じ), the same particle used for 'A と B' togetherness — not the quotative or conditional と."
notes:
  - text: "違{ちが}う takes a different particle — Aと違{ちが}って ('unlike A'), the natural antonym of Aと同{おな}じで."
