equivalents:
  - "while / during ~ — something happens at a point *within* a span of time (with に); without に, throughout the whole span"
nuance: "〜間{あいだ}に marks a time span and says the main action happens at *some point inside* it — typically a one-time event: 留守{るす}の間{あいだ}に泥棒{どろぼう}が入{はい}った ('a burglar got in while I was out'). Drop the に (〜間{あいだ}) and the main action instead lasts the *whole* span: 留守{るす}の間{あいだ}、犬{いぬ}が吠{ほ}えていた ('the dog barked the entire time I was out'). The に is the single most important choice here."

contrasts:

    label: "うちに"
    distinction: "間{あいだ}に just locates an event within a span; うちに adds 'before the chance/state is gone' — do it now while conditions still hold (熱{あつ}いうちに食{た}べる, 'eat it while it's hot'). 間{あいだ}に carries no such urgency."
