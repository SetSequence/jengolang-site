  - attaches_to: "verb"

restrictions:
  - text: "Attaches to the **stem**, not the plain form — and that is the whole difference from hearsay そうだ. 降{ふ}りそうだ (stem) = 'it looks about to rain'; 降{ふ}るそうだ (plain form) = 'I hear it'll rain.' Watch the attachment."
  - text: "Use it for things you can perceive right now. For a reasoned guess from indirect evidence, use ようだ／みたい instead (足音{あしおと}がする。誰{だれ}か来{き}たようだ, not 来{き}そうだ)."
variants:
  - form: "よさそう / なさそう"
    note: "Irregular stems: いい → よさそう, ない → なさそう (an extra さ is inserted). The adverbial/adnominal forms are そうに・そうな (see 〜そうに・〜そうな)."
contrasts:
  - slug: "souda"
    label: "そうだ (hearsay)"
    distinction: "The minimal pair: 〜そうだ (appearance) takes the **stem** (おいしそう, 降{ふ}りそう); 伝聞{でんぶん}の そうだ takes the **plain form** (おいしいそう, 降{ふ}るそう) and reports what you heard."
  - slug: "youda"
    label: "ようだ"
    distinction: "そうだ = an immediate impression of how something looks now; ようだ = a reasoned inference from evidence. 降{ふ}りそう = the sky looks like rain; 降{ふ}ったようだ = the ground is wet, so it must have rained."
  - slug: "ni-mieru"
    label: "〜に見える"
    distinction: "〜に見える states what something visually resembles (本物{ほんもの}に見える = looks like the real thing); 〜そうだ judges a quality or imminent event (おいしそう = looks tasty)."
