restrictions:
  - text: "Doesn't apply when a verb has its own suppletive honorific — 来{く}る/行{い}く/いる → いらっしゃる, する → なさる, 見{み}る → ご覧{らん}になる. ×お来{こ}になる is wrong. Single-mora stems (見る→お見になる) are also blocked, so those rely on the lexical forms."
  - text: "Honorific, so only for the subject's action — never for your own. ×私がお読みになる."
contrasts:

  - slug: "irassharu"

    distinction: "For 来る/行く/いる you cannot build お〜になる — the suppletive いらっしゃる replaces the whole pattern."
  - slug: "nasaru"
    label: "なさる"
    distinction: "For する specifically, use the lexical honorific なさる rather than forcing お〜になる."
