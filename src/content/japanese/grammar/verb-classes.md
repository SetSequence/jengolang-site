formation:
  - attaches_to: "Godan (u-verb, 五段)"
    form: "stem ends in a consonant; the final syllable shifts across the う-row"
    example: "飲{の}む → 飲{の}みます、書{か}く → 書{か}かない、話{はな}す → 話{はな}した"
  - attaches_to: "Ichidan (ru-verb, 一段)"
    form: "ends in an え／い-sound + る; drop る and attach the ending"
    example: "食{た}べる → 食{た}べます、見{み}る → 見{み}ない、寝{ね}る → 寝{ね}た"
  - attaches_to: "Irregular"
    form: "only する and 来{く}る — memorize their shapes"
    example: "する → します・しない・した、来{く}る → 来{き}ます・来{こ}ない・来{き}た"
usageSetting: "A dictionary-form verb ending in anything other than る is always godan (飲む, 書く, 話す). A verb ending in る may be either, so the え／い-vowel test is the everyday shortcut — with a short list of exceptions to memorize."
restrictions:
  - text: "Some common verbs end in る but are godan, not ichidan: 帰{かえ}る, 入{はい}る, 走{はし}る, 要{い}る, 知{し}る, 切{き}る. They conjugate 帰{かえ}ります／帰{かえ}らない — the え／い-vowel test misfires here, so learn these as exceptions."
