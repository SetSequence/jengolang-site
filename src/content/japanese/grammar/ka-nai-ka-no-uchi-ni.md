equivalents:
  - "no sooner had ~ than / had barely ~ when — the second event happens before the first one has even finished"
nuance: "Built by repeating the verb — once in dictionary form before か, once in ない-form before かのうちに — so it literally asks 'whether ~ or not-~' at the exact moment of the boundary. The picture is two events overlapping so tightly you can't tell if the first actually completed before the second began. It is more vivid and immediate than 〜たとたん, and belongs to narrative/written Japanese. Both clauses are past, observed facts: the second clause can't be a command, request, or the speaker's own plan."
keySentence:
  jp: "ベルが鳴{な}るか鳴{な}らないかのうちに、生徒{せいと}たちは教室{きょうしつ}を飛{と}び出{だ}した。"
  en: "No sooner had the bell rung than the students bolted out of the classroom."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Same verb twice: dictionary-form + か, then ない-form + かのうちに"
    form: "Vる + か + Vない + かのうちに"
    example: "着{つ}く → 着{つ}くか着{つ}かないかのうちに"
usageSetting: "Narrative or reportive; describes something the speaker witnessed. The second clause states what then happened — not an instruction or intention."
examples:
  - jp: "彼{かれ}は席{せき}に着{つ}くか着{つ}かないかのうちに、話{はな}し始{はじ}めた。"
    en: "He began talking almost before he'd even sat down."
  - jp: "「おやすみ」と言{い}うか言{い}わないかのうちに、子{こ}どもは眠{ねむ}ってしまった。"
    en: "No sooner had the child said 'goodnight' than she was fast asleep."
  - jp: "家{いえ}を出{で}るか出{で}ないかのうちに、雨{あめ}が降{ふ}り出{だ}した。"
    en: "I had barely stepped out of the house when it started to rain."
contrasts:
  - slug: "ta-totan"
    label: "〜た途端{とたん}"
    distinction: "た途端{とたん} marks the instant *after* the first action finishes (ドアを開{あ}けた途端{とたん}); か〜ないかのうちに says the second event arrives *before the first even finishes* — an even tighter, more literary overlap."
  - slug: "ya-ina-ya"
    label: "〜や否{いな}や"
    distinction: "Both are literary 'the instant ~'. や否{いな}や takes one verb and stresses an immediate next step; か〜ないかのうちに repeats the verb to stress that you can't tell whether the first action completed at all."
  - slug: "ka-to-omou-to"
    label: "〜かと思{おも}うと"
    distinction: "かと思{おも}うと adds the speaker's mild surprise that a *contrasting* thing follows; か〜ないかのうちに is purely about the razor-thin timing gap, with no contrast implied."
notes:
  - text: "The repeated verb must be the same one (鳴{な}る→鳴{な}らない, 着{つ}く→着{つ}かない). It is a fixed frame — don't insert other material between the two halves."
