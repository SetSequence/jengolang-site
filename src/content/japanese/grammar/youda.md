contrasts:

  - slug: "rashii"
    label: "〜らしい"
    distinction: "らしい leans on hearsay or what's typical and feels less personal; ようだ is the speaker's direct impression. らしい: 'apparently'; ようだ: 'it looks to me like'."
    sense: "conjecture from evidence"
  - slug: "mitai"
    label: "〜みたい"
    distinction: "みたい is the casual spoken equivalent of ようだ for both senses (雨{あめ}が降{ふ}ったみたい). ようだ is neutral-to-formal and the written choice."
