  - attaches_to: "Verb / adjective directly"
    form: "no relative pronoun needed"
    example: "走{はし}っている人{ひと} (the running person)"
examples:
  - jp: "日本語{にほんご}が話{はな}せる人{ひと}を探{さが}している。"

    en: "I bought bread at the shop in front of the station."
restrictions:
  - text: "Inside the modifying clause, the subject takes が or の, not は: 私{わたし}が読{よ}んだ本{ほん} / 私{わたし}の読{よ}んだ本{ほん} ✓, ×私{わたし}は読{よ}んだ本{ほん}. The verb or adjective is in plain form and always comes directly before the noun."
notes:
  - text: "Unlike English, there is no relative pronoun (who/which/that) and no change of word order — the clause simply sits in front of the noun it describes."
