equivalents:
  - "because of ~ / owing to ~ — links a cause to an undesirable result, with a note of blame"
nuance: "せいで states a cause and blames it for a bad outcome that follows: 雨{あめ}のせいで試合{しあい}が中止{ちゅうし}になった ('because of the rain, the match was cancelled'). Unlike neutral から/ので, せいで carries clear negative valence — the result is unwanted and the cause is at fault. Its positive twin is おかげで. For an uncertain cause, せいか ('perhaps because')."
keySentence:
  jp: "事故{じこ}のせいで、電車{でんしゃ}が遅{おく}れた。"
  en: "Because of an accident, the train was delayed."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun + の / plain clause / na-adj な / i-adj + せいで"
    form: "Nの / 〜 plain + せいで"
    example: "渋滞{じゅうたい}のせいで / 食{た}べ過{す}ぎたせいで"
examples:
  - jp: "君{きみ}のせいで、計画{けいかく}が台無{だいな}しだ。"
    en: "Because of you, the plan is ruined."
  - jp: "風邪{かぜ}のせいで、仕事{しごと}を休{やす}んだ。"
    en: "Because of a cold, I took the day off work."
  - jp: "暑{あつ}さのせいで、よく眠{ねむ}れなかった。"
    en: "Owing to the heat, I couldn't sleep well."
contrasts:
  - slug: "okage-de"
    label: "〜おかげで"
    distinction: "Same structure, opposite valence: せいで blames a *bad* result on the cause; おかげで credits a *good* one. 雨{あめ}のせいで (bad) vs 君{きみ}のおかげで (good)."
  - slug: "kara"
    label: "〜から"
    distinction: "から/ので state a reason neutrally; せいで adds blame and signals the result is unwelcome. Use から for a plain 'because', せいで to fault the cause."
notes:
  - text: "せいか replaces せいで when the cause is uncertain: 緊張{きんちょう}のせいか、声{こえ}が震{ふる}えた ('perhaps from nerves, my voice shook')."
