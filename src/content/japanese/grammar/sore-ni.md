    en: "He's kind, and what's more, he's smart."
restrictions:
  - text: "それに piles on a point in the SAME direction (both good, or both bad); it cannot introduce a contrast — for 'and yet' use それなのに, for a plain 'but' use しかし."
contrasts:
  - slug: "soshite"
    label: "そして"
    distinction: "そして chains events in sequence ('and then'); それに simply adds another supporting point ('and besides'), with no sense of time order."
  - slug: "sono-ue"
    label: "その上"
    distinction: "Both add 'on top of that'. それに is the everyday spoken form; その上{うえ} is a touch more formal and often marks the added point as more striking."
  - slug: "sore-nanoni"
    label: "それなのに"
    distinction: "それに adds something in the same direction; それなのに flips direction to a result that contradicts the expectation."
notes:
  - text: "Very common in casual speech to stack reasons: 高{たか}いし、それに遠{とお}い ('it's expensive, and besides it's far')."
