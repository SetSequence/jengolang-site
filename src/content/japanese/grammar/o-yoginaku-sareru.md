equivalents:
  - "be forced to ~ / be compelled into ~ / have no choice but to ~ (because of circumstances) — formal/written; the affected party is the subject and the outcome is unwanted"
keySentence:
  jp: "大雨{おおあめ}のため、試合{しあい}は中止{ちゅうし}を余儀{よぎ}なくされた。"
  en: "Because of the heavy rain, the match was forced to be cancelled."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun (usually a する-noun naming the outcome)"
    form: "[affected party]は + N + を余儀{よぎ}なくされる"
    example: "閉鎖{へいさ}を余儀{よぎ}なくされる"
examples:
  - jp: "工場{こうじょう}は経営難{けいえいなん}で閉鎖{へいさ}を余儀{よぎ}なくされた。"
    en: "Financial trouble forced the factory to close."
  - jp: "住民{じゅうみん}は災害{さいがい}で避難{ひなん}を余儀{よぎ}なくされた。"
    en: "The disaster forced residents to evacuate."
  - jp: "けがのため、選手{せんしゅ}は引退{いんたい}を余儀{よぎ}なくされた。"
    en: "The injury left the athlete no choice but to retire."
restrictions:
  - text: "を takes a NOUN (typically a する-noun: 中止・変更・閉鎖・撤退・延期), never a plain verb. The compelling force is impersonal circumstance, not a person giving an order; the subject is the one suffering the compulsion."
contrasts:
  - slug: "o-yoginaku-saseru"
    label: "〜を余儀{よぎ}なくさせる"
    distinction: "される makes the VICTIM the subject ('Y was forced into ~'); させる makes the CAUSE the subject ('X forced Y into ~'). Same situation, flipped focus — and the passive される is far more common in writing."
  - slug: "zaru-o-enai"
    label: "〜ざるを得ない"
    distinction: "を余儀なくされる takes a noun and is heavier, news/report register, stressing that circumstances strip away all options; ざるを得ない attaches to a verb and is the everyday written 'have no choice but to ~.'"
  - slug: "nakereba-naranai"
    label: "〜なければならない"
    distinction: "なければならない = an obligation from rule or necessity that you still carry out; を余儀なくされる = circumstances impose an unwanted outcome on you, foregrounding the loss of choice."
