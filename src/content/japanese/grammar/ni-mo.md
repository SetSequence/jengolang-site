restrictions:
  - text: "With a question word + negative, にも means 'not ... to anyone/anywhere' (誰{だれ}にも言{い}わない 'I won't tell anyone'). The も here is the negative-sweep も, not a casual 'also'."
contrasts:
  - slug: "mo"
    label: "も"
    distinction: "Bare も replaces が/を; にも keeps に (a dative/locative target) and adds も on top — 'to/at ~ as well'. Use にも when the role being added is an indirect object, location, or time, not a subject/object."
notes:
  - text: "にも is just a particle stack (に + も); it is not a fixed grammar point. The meaning follows directly from whatever に is doing in the sentence, plus も's 'also / even'."
