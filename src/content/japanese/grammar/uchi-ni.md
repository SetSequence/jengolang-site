nuance: "うちに frames a state as temporary and says 'act within it'. With a positive state it's 'while it still holds' (明{あか}るいうちに); with a negative it's 'before the opposite sets in' (忘{わす}れないうちに = before forgetting). A separate use, 〜ているうちに, reports a gradual, unplanned change that emerges as the action continues. The common thread is a window that won't stay open."
