usageSetting: "Casual speech only. Rising intonation = a gentle question (どうしたの？); falling = a soft explanation. The falling-tone statement form sounds notably feminine; men more often use んだ."
contrasts:

notes:
  - text: "Don't tag の onto every casual sentence — like のだ, it implies there's a reason or circumstance behind the statement, not a flat report."
