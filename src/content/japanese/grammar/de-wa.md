contrasts:
  - slug: "de"
    label: "で"
    distinction: "Plain で marks place/means neutrally (東京{とうきょう}で働{はたら}く). Adding は (では) sets that scope against an unspoken alternative — 'in Tokyo, as opposed to elsewhere.'"
  - slug: "wa"
    label: "は"
    distinction: "は topicalizes a noun directly. では keeps the で role (place/means) and layers contrastive は on top, so the scope itself is what's being contrasted."
notes:
