canonical: "など"
reading: "など"

keigo: "none"
family: "particle"
freq: "common"
jlpt: "N4"
confidence: "high"
senses:
  - label: "and so on"
    equivalents:
      - "~ and so on / ~ etc. / things like ~ — marks the listed item(s) as examples, not a complete set"
    keySentence:
      jp: "果物{くだもの}、特{とく}にりんごやみかんなどが好{す}きだ。"
      en: "I like fruit, especially apples, oranges, and so on."
    examples:
      - jp: "辞書{じしょ}やノートなどを持{も}ってきてください。"
        en: "Please bring a dictionary, notebook, and the like."
      - jp: "東京{とうきょう}や大阪{おおさか}などの大都市{だいとし}。"
        en: "Big cities such as Tokyo and Osaka."
  - label: "belittling / dismissive"
    equivalents:
      - "(the likes of) ~ / ~ of all things — downplays or dismisses the marked item, often modestly or scornfully"
    keySentence:
      jp: "私{わたし}などにはもったいないです。"
      en: "It's wasted on someone like me."
    examples:
      - jp: "お礼{れい}など要{い}りませんよ。"
        en: "There's no need for thanks or anything."
      - jp: "君{きみ}の助{たす}けなど要{い}らない。"
        en: "I don't need help from the likes of you."
nuance: "など primarily flags the listed item as just an example ('…and so on'). The same particle can also belittle — attached to oneself it's humble (私{わたし}など), attached to others or a thing it can be dismissive (〜など要{い}らない). Tone and context decide which. 〜なんか / 〜なんて are its more colloquial variants."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Nouns (often after a や list); also after verbs in writing"
    form: "(N や) N など / 〜たりなど"
    example: "コーヒーや紅茶{こうちゃ}など / 心配{しんぱい}などしない"
contrasts:
  - slug: "ya"
    label: "や"
    distinction: "They team up: や opens a representative noun list, など closes it with 'and so on'. や joins the items; など labels the whole list as non-exhaustive."
  - slug: "toka"
    label: "とか"
    distinction: "Both say 'examples, not the full set', but とか is casual/spoken; など is more neutral-to-formal and is the standard choice in writing."
notes:
  - text: "Colloquial なんか and emphatic なんて share the belittling/example function: お金{かね}なんかいらない ('I don't need money or anything')."
