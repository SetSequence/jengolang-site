  - "whether to ~ or not — wavering between doing something and not doing it, about one's own action"
nuance: "Pairs the volitional 〜ようか ('shall I ~?') with its negative まい ('or not ~?') to describe the speaker agonising over a decision that is theirs to make. 留学{りゅうがく}しようか(し)まいか、ずっと迷{まよ}っている ('I keep agonising over whether or not to study abroad'). It typically feeds a verb of deliberation — 迷{まよ}う・悩{なや}む・考{かんが}える. Because it uses the volitional, the choice is always about the speaker's *own* intended action, not a yes/no fact."
