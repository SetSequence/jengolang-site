formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun (and some particles)"
    form: "N + しか + negative predicate"
    example: "水{みず}しかない / 君{きみ}にしか言{い}わない"
usageSetting: "Replaces は・が・を; stacks after other particles (にしか, からしか). The verb that follows must be negative."
examples:
  - jp: "一人{ひとり}しか来{こ}なかった。"
    en: "Only one person came (and that was disappointing)."
  - jp: "日本語{にほんご}でしか話{はな}せません。"
    en: "I can only speak in Japanese."
  - jp: "今{いま}はこれしかできない。"
    en: "This is all I can do right now."
restrictions:
  - text: "しか cannot stand with a positive verb. ×百円{ひゃくえん}しかある is wrong — the predicate must be negative (百円{ひゃくえん}しかない). The negative is part of the pattern, even though the meaning ('only') feels positive in English."
contrasts:
  - slug: "dake"
    label: "〜だけ"
    distinction: "だけ is a neutral limit and takes a positive verb (百円{ひゃくえん}だけある). しか forces a negative and adds the 'only that much, not enough' feeling (百円{ひゃくえん}しかない)."
  - slug: "bakari"
    label: "〜ばかり"
    distinction: "しか = 'nothing but ~, and too little'; ばかり = 'nothing but ~' in the sense of too much / one-sided repetition (お菓子{かし}ばかり食{た}べる)."
