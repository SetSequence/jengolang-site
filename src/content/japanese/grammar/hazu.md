restrictions:
  - text: "はず is grounded in logic or evidence, not in the speaker's own will. For your own plan use つもり, not はず: ×明日{あした}行{い}くはず (for 'I intend to go') → 明日{あした}行{い}くつもり."
contrasts:
  - slug: "darou"
    label: "だろう"
    distinction: "はず claims an expectation backed by a reason ('it should be, because…'); だろう is a looser guess that needs no grounds."
  - slug: "kamoshirenai"
    label: "〜かもしれない"
    distinction: "はず = confident, evidence-based expectation; かもしれない = mere possibility. はず is much closer to certainty."
notes:
  - text: "When reality contradicts the expectation, use はずだ in surprise — 「おかしいな、ここにあるはずなのに」('strange, it's supposed to be here')."
