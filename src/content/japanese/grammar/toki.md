    en: "When I went to Japan (once there), I bought a camera."
    note: "行{い}ったとき — past: action after arriving"
restrictions:
  - text: "The tense of the clause before とき sets the timing: V-る とき = the main action happens before / on the way to the clause action; V-た とき = after it is complete. 寝{ね}るとき (before sleeping) vs 寝{ね}たとき (after falling asleep)."
notes:
  - text: "Written either as 時 or in kana とき; the kana form is common when it's grammatical rather than the literal noun 'time'."
