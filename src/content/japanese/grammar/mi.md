restrictions:
  - text: "〜み is NOT productive — it forms a fixed set of words (重{おも}み, 深{ふか}み, 痛{いた}み, 甘{あま}み, 強{つよ}み, 弱{よわ}み, 温{あたた}かみ). You can't coin 〜み from just any adjective the way you can with 〜さ."
contrasts:
  - slug: "sa"
    label: "〜さ"
    distinction: "Both nominalize adjectives, but 〜さ gives an objective, measurable degree (高{たか}さ = 'height — how tall?'), while 〜み gives a subjective, felt quality (高{たか}み = 'a height / lofty feel'). 〜さ attaches to virtually any adjective; 〜み to a limited, lexicalized set."

    en: "His paintings have a distinctive warmth."
notes:
  - text: "Many 〜み nouns are lexicalized with their own meaning: 楽{たの}しみ ('a pleasure / something to look forward to'), 強{つよ}み・弱{よわ}み ('strong/weak point')."
