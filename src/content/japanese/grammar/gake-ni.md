  - "on the way ~ / just as one is ~ing — doing something incidentally at the point of a movement"
nuance: "がけ attaches to a small fixed set of motion-verb stems — 出{で}がけ ('on one's way out'), 帰{かえ}りがけ ('on the way home'), 行{い}きがけ ('on the way there'), 通{とお}りがけ ('while passing by') — and + に marks doing something else at that point of the trip. 帰{かえ}りがけに、本屋{ほんや}に寄{よ}った ('on the way home, I stopped by the bookshop'). The incidental action happens *during/at the moment of* that movement, not as a planned detour."
