equivalents:
  - "should be ~ / is supposed to ~ / ought to be ~ — a confident expectation the speaker reasons out from known facts"
keySentence:
  jp: "彼{かれ}はもう着{つ}いているはずだ。"
  en: "He should have arrived by now."
examples:
  - jp: "予約{よやく}したから、席{せき}はあるはずだ。"
    en: "I made a reservation, so there should be seats."
  - jp: "彼女{かのじょ}は日本{にほん}で育{そだ}ったから、漢字{かんじ}が読{よ}めるはずだ。"
    en: "She grew up in Japan, so she should be able to read kanji."
  - jp: "約束{やくそく}は三時{さんじ}だったはずだけど。"
    en: "The plan was supposed to be three o'clock, I thought."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "plain verb / i-adjective"
    form: "V/A(plain) + はずだ"
    example: "着{つ}いているはずだ / 高{たか}いはずだ"
  - attaches_to: "na-adjective / noun"
    form: "na-adj + な + はずだ; N + の + はずだ"
    example: "静{しず}かなはずだ / 休{やす}みのはずだ"
restrictions:
  - text: "はず is an expectation the speaker deduces — not a duty. 'You should study' as advice is 勉強{べんきょう}するべきだ, not ✗勉強{べんきょう}するはずだ (which would mean 'he's expected to study')."
variants:
  - form: "〜はずだった"
    note: "〜はずだった = 'was supposed to ~ (but it didn't happen).' 今日{きょう}届{とど}くはずだったのに。 — 'It was supposed to arrive today, but...'"
contrasts:
  - slug: "beki-da"
    label: "〜べきだ"
    distinction: "The classic mix-up. はずだ = expectation ('I expect it's so'); べきだ = obligation ('it's the right thing to do'). 来{く}るはずだ = I expect him to come; 来{く}るべきだ = he ought to come."
  - slug: "ni-chigainai"
    label: "〜に違いない"
    distinction: "はずだ = a reasoned 'should be' that allows for being wrong; に違いない = near-certain conviction ('must be, I'm sure'). はず is logic; ちがいない is confidence."
  - slug: "darou"
    label: "だろう"
    distinction: "だろう is a general guess ('probably'); はずだ is grounded in a specific reason the speaker can point to ('he booked it, so there should be a seat')."
  - slug: "wake-da"
    label: "わけだ"
    distinction: "はずだ looks forward to what should be true; わけだ looks back and explains a known fact ('no wonder ~'). Expectation vs explanation."
