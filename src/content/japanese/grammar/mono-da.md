    label: "〜べきだ"

  - slug: "hazu-da"
    label: "〜はずだ"
    distinction: "はずだ is a logical expectation drawn from specific evidence ('it should be the case that ~'). ものだ states a general truth or social norm, not a deduction about a particular situation."
