equivalents:
  - "both ~ and ~ (affirmative) / neither ~ nor ~ (with negative) — marks two or more parallel items as alike under one predicate"
nuance: "Repeating も puts two items in parallel and says the predicate applies to all of them equally: 兄{あに}も弟{おとうと}も来{き}た ('both my older and younger brother came'). With a negative predicate it flips to 'neither…nor': お酒{さけ}もタバコもやらない ('I neither drink nor smoke'). The emphasis is on the items being the same in this respect — stronger and more parallel than a neutral と list."

  en: "I can speak both English and French."
formation:

    form: "N も N も (＋ predicate)"

    en: "He eats neither meat nor fish."
  - jp: "父{ちち}も母{はは}も元気{げんき}です。"
    en: "Both my father and mother are well."
contrasts:
  - slug: "to-2"
    label: "と (listing)"
    distinction: "と neutrally joins an exhaustive list ('A and B'); 〜も〜も adds 'alike / equally', stressing that the predicate holds for each item the same way — and uniquely supports the 'neither…nor' negative reading."
  - slug: "mo"
    label: "も"
    distinction: "Single も means 'also/too' for one item (私{わたし}も = 'me too'); repeating it (〜も〜も) coordinates two or more items as 'both…and' / 'neither…nor'."
notes:
