equivalents:
  - "compared to ~ / in comparison with ~ — sets a baseline against which a difference is measured"
nuance: "〜に比{くら}べて marks the thing you're measuring against, then states how the subject differs: 去年{きょねん}に比{くら}べて売上{うりあげ}が増{ふ}えた ('sales rose compared to last year'). Unlike より, which directly ranks two things ('A is bigger than B'), に比{くら}べて frames a baseline and is common with changes, statistics, and broad tendencies. Slightly formal; everyday speech often uses より."
keySentence:
  jp: "去年{きょねん}に比{くら}べて、今年{ことし}は雨{あめ}が多{おお}い。"
  en: "Compared to last year, there's been more rain this year."

  - jp: "去年{きょねん}に比{くら}べて、人口{じんこう}が減{へ}っている。"

contrasts:
  - slug: "yori"
    label: "〜より"
    distinction: "より directly ranks two items ('A より B のほうが ~'); に比{くら}べて sets a *baseline* and describes the gap from it — better for trends and statistics, and it doesn't need a 'winner'."
  - slug: "ni-kuraberu-to"
    label: "に比べると"
    distinction: "Near-identical; に比{くら}べると frames it as a slight conditional ('when you compare it to ~') and leans a touch more tentative, while に比{くら}べて is the plainer 'compared to'."
notes:
