restrictions:
  - text: "A clause takes only one を. With desire (〜たい) and potential forms the object often switches to が: 水{みず}を飲{の}みたい and 水{みず}が飲{の}みたい are both natural — but you cannot stack two を on one verb."
contrasts:
  - slug: "ga-2"
    label: "が"
    distinction: "を marks the plain direct object; with 〜たい / potential / わかる the same noun is frequently re-marked with が because those predicates describe a state rather than an action (本{ほん}を読{よ}む → 本{ほん}が読{よ}みたい)."
notes:
  - text: "を also marks the path traversed by a motion verb (公園{こうえん}を歩{ある}く) and the place left behind (家{いえ}を出{で}る) — covered as separate uses of を."
