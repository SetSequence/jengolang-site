contrasts:
  - slug: "ne"
    label: "ね"
    distinction: "ね neutrally invites agreement and works in polite speech (ですね); sentence-final な is rougher, more self-directed, and casual-only — closer to thinking aloud."
  - slug: "naa"
    label: "なあ"
    distinction: "なあ is the drawn-out, more emotional version of this な — stronger admiration or longing. Plain な is shorter and flatter."
notes:
  - text: "Do not confuse with the **prohibitive** な, which attaches to a verb's dictionary form and means 'don't': 行{い}くな = 'don't go'. That な makes a command; this sentence-final な expresses feeling after a complete statement."
