restrictions:
  - text: "からだ ends a sentence to supply the reason for what was just stated, so it stands after the result, not before it. To attach a reason inside a single sentence ('because X, Y'), use plain から/ので instead."
contrasts:
  - slug: "kara"
    label: "から"
    distinction: "Embedded から links reason and result in one sentence (遅{おく}れたから謝{あやま}った). からだ closes a separate sentence that explains a previously stated fact ('… It's because ~')."
  - slug: "nazenara"
    label: "なぜなら"
    distinction: "なぜなら opens the explanation ('the reason is…') and typically closes with からだ. They work as a pair: なぜなら … からだ."
  - slug: "wake-da"
    label: "わけだ"
    distinction: "わけだ draws a conclusion that makes sense of a situation ('that's why ~, no wonder'). からだ states the cause itself ('it's because ~'); わけだ states the resulting realization."
