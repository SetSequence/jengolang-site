equivalents:
  - "thanks to ~ / owing to ~ — a noun crediting a good result to a person or cause"
nuance: "おかげ is the noun for the help or cause you owe a good outcome to. As 〜のおかげだ it gives credit (あなたのおかげだ, 'it's thanks to you'); the set phrase おかげさまで ('thanks to you / I'm fine, thank you') is a staple of polite exchange. Its valence is *positive* — the mirror image of せい ('fault'). The connective form is おかげで."

  en: "It's thanks to you, teacher, that I passed."

contrasts:
