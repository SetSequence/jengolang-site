  - "I'm sorry for ~ing — apologizes for an action that caused trouble; also 'thank you for ~ing' when someone went out of their way for you"
nuance: "〜てすみません attaches すみません to a て-form cause: 遅{おく}れてすみません ('sorry for being late'). Because すみません covers both apology and thanks, 〜てすみません can also mean 'thank you for (the trouble of) ~ing' — わざわざ来{き}てくれてすみません ('sorry/thanks for coming all this way'). Negative cause uses なくて: 連絡{れんらく}できなくてすみません ('sorry I couldn't reach you'). More formal: 〜て申{もう}し訳{わけ}ありません."
