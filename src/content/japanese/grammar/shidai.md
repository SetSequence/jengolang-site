contrasts:

    distinction: "たらすぐ is the everyday 'as soon as' and works for past or future; ます-stem + 次第 is the formal version, restricted to a not-yet-done action followed by a planned next step."
  - slug: "shidai-de"
    label: "〜次第で"
    distinction: "次第だ (this page) says the result *depends entirely on* X as a predicate; 次第で adds で to say 'depending on X, the outcome varies' adverbially before a clause."
  - slug: "ni-yoru"
    label: "による"
    distinction: "による / によって also express 'depending on'; 次第 is more personal and decisive ('it's all up to ~'), while による is the neutral 'varies according to ~'."
notes:
