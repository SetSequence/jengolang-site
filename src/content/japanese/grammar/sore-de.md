restrictions:
  - text: "それで links a result to a cause already stated as a separate sentence; it does not join two clauses inside one sentence the way ので/から do (×遅刻したそれで → use 〜て or 〜ので)."
contrasts:
  - slug: "dakara"
    label: "だから"
    distinction: "Both give 'so/therefore', but だから foregrounds the speaker's logic/assertion ('so, naturally…'), while それで just neutrally narrates what followed."
  - slug: "sore-nara"
    label: "それなら"
    distinction: "それで takes the prior statement as a real cause ('and so…'); それなら takes it as a condition to respond to ('if that's the case, then…')."
notes:
  - text: "On its own as a prompt, それで？ means 'And then? / So what happened?', urging the speaker to continue."
