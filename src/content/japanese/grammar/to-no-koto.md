equivalents:
  - "I'm told that ~ / word is that ~ / according to what I heard ~ — relays a message received, politely and at a remove"
nuance: "Sentence-ending との + こと(です) that relays information the speaker was given by someone else, without vouching for it personally. It's the polite, business-correspondence way to pass on a message: 部長{ぶちょう}は明日{あした}出張{しゅっちょう}とのことです ('I'm told the manager is away on business tomorrow'). Because it frames the content as a received report, it keeps the speaker at a courteous distance from it — the staple of relaying email and phone messages."
keySentence:

  en: "I'm told Mr. Tanaka will arrive at three."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Clause (plain) + とのこと(だ/です); also Nだ → Nとのこと"
    form: "[plain clause] + とのことだ / とのことです"
    example: "延期{えんき}になった → 延期{えんき}になったとのことです"
usageSetting: "Polite relaying of second-hand information — business emails, phone messages, passing on what someone else said. The speaker is the messenger, not the source."
examples:
  - jp: "先方{せんぽう}は、その条件{じょうけん}で問題{もんだい}ないとのことでした。"
    en: "The other party said there was no problem with those terms."
  - jp: "お電話{でんわ}がありまして、明日{あした}は来{こ}られないとのことです。"
    en: "There was a call — I'm told they can't come tomorrow."
  - jp: "天気予報{てんきよほう}によると、週末{しゅうまつ}は晴{は}れるとのことだ。"
    en: "According to the forecast, it's going to be clear this weekend."
contrasts:
  - slug: "souda"
    label: "そうだ"
    distinction: "そうだ (hearsay) plainly states 'I hear that ~'; とのこと frames it as a *message received and relayed*, more polite and at a remove — the choice for passing on what a third party communicated."
  - slug: "to-iu-koto-da"
    label: "ということだ"
    distinction: "ということだ has two jobs — hearsay 'I hear ~' and conclusion 'that means ~'. とのこと only does the relayed-hearsay job, and does it more deferentially."
  - slug: "to-no"
    label: "との"
    distinction: "Same root, different position: との modifies a following noun ('the report that ~'); とのこと ends the sentence as relayed hearsay ('I'm told that ~')."
notes:
  - text: "Very common in business Japanese to close a relayed message; 〜とのことでございます is the extra-polite (keigo) version."
