restrictions:
  - text: "もん is sentence-final and emotionally coloured — it justifies oneself, so it doesn't fit neutral explanation in formal or written contexts. There, use から or ので."
contrasts:
  - slug: "kara"
    label: "から"

  - slug: "datte"
    label: "だって"
    distinction: "だって opens the excuse ('but…') at the front of the sentence; もん closes it at the end. They frequently pair up: だって〜もん."
notes:
  - text: "もん is just the casual contraction of sentence-final もの. The full もの is slightly softer/more feminine; もん is more clipped and conversational."
