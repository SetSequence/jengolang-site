equivalents:
  - "how much? / how many? — the question word for price or amount"
nuance: "いくら is the quantity question word — most often a price (これはいくら？) but also any amount or number. The reading いくつ is for counting discrete things ('how many'); いくら leans toward money and uncountable amounts. Fronted before a 〜ても/〜でも clause it shifts into the concessive 'no matter how much' (see いくら〜ても), but on its own it simply asks an amount."

contrasts:

    label: "いくら〜ても"
    distinction: "Front いくら onto a 〜ても clause and it stops asking a price and means 'no matter how much ~' — the concessive use is its own pattern; bare いくら just asks an amount."
  - slug: "ikura-demo"
    label: "いくら〜でも"
    distinction: "いくらでも (fused) means 'any amount / as much as you like' — an answer of 'unlimited', the flip side of asking いくら 'how much'."
