contrasts:
  - slug: "dake"
    label: "だけ"
    distinction: "だけ is a particle that limits a specific word (これだけ, 'only this'); the 'only' ただ is an adverb setting the whole clause to 'merely', and the two often team up as ただ〜だけ for emphasis."
  - slug: "shikashi"
    label: "しかし"
    distinction: "しかし introduces a full contrast or objection; the 'however' ただ adds just one minor reservation to an otherwise positive statement — lighter, like 'the one catch is…'."
notes:
