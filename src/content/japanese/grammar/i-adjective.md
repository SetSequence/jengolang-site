keigo: "none"
freq: "essential"

family: "adjective"
prereqs: []
related: ["na-adjective", "kute", "ni-naru"]
confidence: "high"
noindex: false
equivalents:
  - "adjectives ending in 〜い (高{たか}い, 新{あたら}しい) that conjugate by themselves — the tense and the negative live in the adjective, not in a copula"
nuance: "An い-adjective is a mini-predicate: 高{たか}い already means \"is expensive,\" so it needs no です to make a sentence. Politeness is added on top; the grammar — negative, past — is carried inside the word."
keySentence:
  jp: "この店{みせ}は安{やす}い。"
  en: "This shop is cheap."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Present (dictionary)"
    form: "〜い"
    example: "高{たか}い (is expensive)"
  - attaches_to: "Negative"
    form: "〜い → 〜くない"
    example: "高{たか}くない (isn't expensive)"
  - attaches_to: "Past"
    form: "〜い → 〜かった"
    example: "高{たか}かった (was expensive)"
  - attaches_to: "Past negative"
    form: "〜くない → 〜くなかった"
    example: "高{たか}くなかった (wasn't expensive)"
  - attaches_to: "Adverb"
    form: "〜い → 〜く"
    example: "高{たか}く (high / expensively)"
  - attaches_to: "Polite"
    form: "add です after any plain form"
    example: "高{たか}いです・高{たか}くなかったです"
usageSetting: "Politeness is added by placing です after any plain form (高{たか}いです, 高{たか}くないです); the adjective itself does not change for politeness. Do not use だ with an い-adjective (×高{たか}いだ)."
restrictions:
  - text: "The tense and negative sit in the adjective, never in a copula: past is 高{たか}かった (not ×高{たか}いだった) and negative is 高{たか}くない (not ×高{たか}いじゃない). The じゃない / だった endings belong to nouns and な-adjectives."
  - text: "いい (good) is irregular — it conjugates from the older stem よ: よくない, よかった, よく, never ×いくない / ×いかった. Compounds inherit this: 格好{かっこう}いい → 格好{かっこう}よかった."
contrasts:
  - slug: "na-adjective"
    label: "な形容詞"
    distinction: "い-adjectives conjugate themselves (高{たか}い → 高{たか}かった). な-adjectives keep one fixed shape and let the copula carry tense and negation (静{しず}か → 静{しず}かだった), and add な before a noun."
  - slug: "kute"
    label: "〜くて"
    distinction: "To link an い-adjective to what follows ('cheap and tasty'), the 〜く base takes て → 安{やす}くて. That connecting form is its own point."
examples:
  - jp: "この本{ほん}は高{たか}いです。"
    en: "This book is expensive."
    note: "present + polite です"
  - jp: "その店{みせ}のラーメンはあまり高{たか}くない。"
    en: "The ramen at that shop isn't very expensive."
    note: "negative 〜くない"
  - jp: "昨日{きのう}のテストは難{むずか}しかった。"
    en: "Yesterday's test was hard."
    note: "past 〜かった"
  - jp: "その映画{えいが}は面白{おもしろ}くなかったです。"
    en: "That movie wasn't interesting."
    note: "past-negative + polite"
  - jp: "毎朝{まいあさ}もっと早{はや}く起{お}きる。"
    en: "Every morning I get up earlier."
    note: "adverb 〜く modifying a verb"
notes:
  - text: "'い-adjective' means the final い is part of the word and follows a vowel sound (高{たか}い, 新{あたら}しい). Some な-adjectives also end in い (綺麗{きれい}, 嫌{きら}い) — there the い is inside the stem, so they conjugate the な way (綺麗{きれい}だった, not ×綺麗{きれい}かった)."
