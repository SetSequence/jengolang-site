restrictions:
  - text: "The main clause cannot be a command, request, invitation, or wish — と is only for automatic or inevitable outcomes. For 'when you arrive, call me' you must use たら/ば, not ×着{つ}くと電話{でんわ}してください."
contrasts:
  - slug: "tara"
    label: "たら"
    distinction: "と demands an automatic/repeating result and bans commands in the main clause; たら is the flexible everyday 'if/when' that freely allows requests and intentions."
  - slug: "ba"
    label: "〜ば"
    distinction: "Both express general cause-effect, but と stresses an inevitable, every-time result (押すと開く); ば frames a more hypothetical condition (安ければ買う)."
  - slug: "nara"
    label: "なら"
    distinction: "と reports an objective automatic result; なら responds to something just raised ('if that's the case…') and never expresses an automatic outcome."
notes:
  - text: "Because と reads as 'every time', it's the natural choice for directions, machine operation, and habitual cause-effect."
