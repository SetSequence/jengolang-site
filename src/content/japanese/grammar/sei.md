equivalents:
  - "the fault of ~ / because of ~ (something bad) — a noun blaming an undesirable result on a cause"
nuance: "せい is a noun meaning 'the cause to blame'. As 〜のせいだ it pins a bad outcome on someone or something (君{きみ}のせいだ, 'it's your fault'); 〜のせいか hedges it ('maybe because of ~'). The defining feature is *negative valence* — せい assigns blame, the exact opposite of おかげ ('thanks to'). The connective form is せいで."
keySentence:
  jp: "負{ま}けたのは私{わたし}のせいだ。"
  en: "We lost, and it's my fault."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun + の / plain clause / na-adj な / i-adj + せい"
    form: "Nの / 〜 plain + せい(だ/か)"
    example: "天気{てんき}のせい / 年{とし}のせいか"
examples:
  - jp: "失敗{しっぱい}を人{ひと}のせいにするな。"
    en: "Don't blame your failures on others."
  - jp: "気{き}のせいかもしれない。"
    en: "It might just be my imagination."
  - jp: "寝不足{ねぶそく}のせいか、頭{あたま}が痛{いた}い。"
    en: "Maybe because I'm short on sleep, my head hurts."
contrasts:
  - slug: "okage"
    label: "おかげ"
    distinction: "Polar opposites in valence: せい blames a *bad* result on a cause ('your fault'); おかげ credits a *good* one ('thanks to you')."
  - slug: "sei-de"
    label: "〜せいで"
    distinction: "せい is the noun ('the fault'); せいで is its connective form linking the cause to the bad result that follows ('because of ~, [bad thing happened]')."
notes:
  - text: "〜せいにする = 'blame on ~' (人{ひと}のせいにする). 〜せいか softens to 'perhaps because' when you're unsure of the cause."
