equivalents:
  - "and that too / and what's more / and a ~ one at that — picks up the thing just mentioned and adds a striking detail about it"
nuance: "それも refers back to the item just stated (それ = 'that') and tacks on an intensifying, often surprising specification of *the same thing*. 彼{かれ}は車{くるま}を買{か}った。それも、新車{しんしゃ}を ('he bought a car — and a brand-new one at that'). It doesn't introduce a separate new fact (that's それに); it sharpens or amplifies the one already on the table, usually to express the speaker's surprise or emphasis."
keySentence:
  jp: "彼女{かのじょ}は試験{しけん}に合格{ごうかく}した。それも、トップの成績{せいせき}で。"
  en: "She passed the exam — and at the top of the class, no less."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "After a statement, introducing an added detail about the same item"
    form: "[statement]。それも、[striking detail]。"
    example: "犬{いぬ}を飼{か}い始{はじ}めた。それも三匹{さんびき}も。"
usageSetting: "Adds an emphatic, often unexpected specification of the thing just mentioned; carries the speaker's surprise. The detail may trail off without a full predicate."
examples:
  - jp: "宿題{しゅくだい}を忘{わす}れた。それも、二日{ふつか}続{つづ}けて。"
    en: "I forgot my homework — two days in a row, at that."
  - jp: "彼{かれ}は社長{しゃちょう}になった。それも、わずか三十歳{さんじっさい}で。"
    en: "He became company president — and at just thirty, no less."
contrasts:
  - slug: "sore-ni"
    label: "それに"
    distinction: "それに adds a *separate* further point ('the room is cheap; besides, it's quiet'); それも amplifies *the same* item with a striking detail ('he bought a house — and a huge one at that'). それに widens; それも sharpens."
  - slug: "sara-ni-wa"
    label: "さらには"
    distinction: "さらには escalates to a further, broader item in a list; それも zooms in on the one item already mentioned and intensifies it."
notes:
  - text: "The added detail is frequently emphatic — number + も (三匹{さんびき}も), or a superlative — and often left as a fragment without a closing verb."
