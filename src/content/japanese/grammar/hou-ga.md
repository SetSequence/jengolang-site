nuance: "方{ほう} is a noun meaning 'side / direction', used to single out *one* alternative: 大{おお}きい方{ほう} ('the big one'), 右{みぎ}の方{ほう} ('the right side'). It's the building block under the comparison frame — add が and a quality and it becomes 〜のほうが ('~ is more'); add がいい and it gives advice (〜ほうがいい). On its own it just selects which one you mean."
