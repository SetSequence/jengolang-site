register: ["casual-spoken", "polite-spoken", "written-modern"]
keigo: "none"

family: "particle"
confidence: "high"
sources:

equivalents:
  - "in order to ~ / for ~ing — names a goal, then the main clause says what's needed or how to reach it"
keySentence:
  jp: "健康{けんこう}でいるには、運動{うんどう}が必要{ひつよう}だ。"
  en: "To stay healthy, exercise is necessary."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Verb (dictionary form) + には"
    form: "Vる + には"
    example: "駅{えき}に行{い}くには (to get to the station)"
restrictions:
  - text: "This purpose には takes a dictionary-form verb, and the main clause states a requirement, means, or evaluation — 〜が必要{ひつよう}だ, 〜といい, 〜なければならない, 〜ことだ. (には has other roles too: topic emphasis 私{わたし}には分{わ}からない, or に + は after a destination/time.)"
contrasts:
  - slug: "tame-ni"
    label: "ために"
    distinction: "〜には sets up a goal and the main clause tells you what's *needed or how* to reach it (上手{じょうず}になるには練習{れんしゅう}が必要{ひつよう}). ために states a purpose followed by the *action taken* to achieve it (上手{じょうず}になるために練習{れんしゅう}する). には → condition/means; ために → action."
examples:
  - jp: "日本語{にほんご}が上手{じょうず}になるには、毎日{まいにち}話{はな}すことだ。"
    en: "To get good at Japanese, the thing is to speak every day."
  - jp: "締{し}め切{き}りに間{ま}に合{あ}わせるには、今{いま}始{はじ}めるしかない。"
    en: "To make the deadline, we have no choice but to start now."
notes:
  - text: "This entry covers the purpose 'in order to' use. The same には also emphasizes a topic (私{わたし}にはできない = 'for me, it's impossible') — a separate function."
