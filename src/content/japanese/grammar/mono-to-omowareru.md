nuance: "The passive 思{おも}われる makes the assumption impersonal: not 'I think' but 'it is presumed', as if the conclusion arises on its own from the evidence. ものと combines with it to present a careful, default inference in formal writing. 事故{じこ}は整備不良{せいびふりょう}が原因{げんいん}ものと思{おも}われる ('the accident is presumed to have been caused by poor maintenance'). The staple of reports, news, and academic prose where the writer avoids the first person."
