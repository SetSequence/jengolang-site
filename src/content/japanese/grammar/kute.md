  - "is ~ and ... — links an i-adjective to the clause that follows"

  en: "This room is spacious and bright."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "i-adjective"
    form: "drop final い → add くて"

  - attaches_to: "いい (irregular)"
    form: "よくて"
    example: "いい → よくて"
examples:

    en: "This shop is cheap and tasty."

  - jp: "天気{てんき}がよくて気持{きも}ちがいい。"
    en: "The weather is nice and it feels great."
contrasts:
  - slug: "de-3"
    label: "で (copula te-form)"
    distinction: "くて links an i-adjective (広{ひろ}くて). To link a noun or na-adjective in the same way, use で: 静{しず}かで広{ひろ}い部屋{へや} (a quiet, spacious room)."
  - slug: "nakute"
    label: "なくて"
    distinction: "くて is the positive te-form of an i-adjective. The negative is くなくて: 高{たか}くなくて = 'is not expensive, and ...'."
notes:
  - text: "When the second clause is a feeling or result, くて often carries a 'because' nuance: 寒{さむ}くて死{し}にそう = 'It's so cold (that) I could die.'"
