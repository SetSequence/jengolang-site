nuance: "とすぐに is the と conditional ('natural/inevitable result') plus すぐに: 'the moment X, Y follows right away'. Because the base is と, it describes an automatic or habitual chain and resists commands or invitations in the second clause — 春{はる}になるとすぐに花{はな}が咲{さ}く ('as soon as spring comes, the flowers bloom'). For a planned action or a request, switch the base to たら (たらすぐ)."
