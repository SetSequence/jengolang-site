    label: "のに"
    distinction: "Both mean 'although,' but のに is the everyday spoken concessive with a tinge of complaint/surprise, while ながらも is more literary and attaches to a stem, often pairing a modest concession with a positive main clause (狭{せま}いながらも楽{たの}しい)."
