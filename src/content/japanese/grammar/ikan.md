nuance: "如何{いかん} is a stiff, written noun meaning 'how it is / the state of affairs'. As a predicate it says an outcome rests on the nature of something: 成功{せいこう}は本人{ほんにん}の努力{どりょく}如何{いかん}だ ('success comes down to how hard the person tries'). It is the base for the formal patterns 如何{いかん}で / 如何{いかん}によって ('depending on ~') and 如何{いかん}にかかわらず / 如何{いかん}を問{と}わず ('regardless of ~'). Bureaucratic and literary in flavour."
