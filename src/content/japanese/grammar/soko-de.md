contrasts:
  - slug: "dakara"
    label: "だから"
    distinction: "だから states a logical result or conclusion ('therefore'), and that result can be any kind of outcome; そこで introduces a deliberate *action the speaker took* in response to the situation — almost always a willed move, not a natural consequence."
  - slug: "sore-de"
    label: "それで"
    distinction: "それで is broader 'and so / and then', linking to any following result including feelings or states; そこで specifically marks a purposeful action taken because of the situation."
notes:
  - text: "The second clause normally describes a volitional action — そこで泣{な}いた ('so I cried') is odd because crying isn't a deliberate response. For such results use それで."
