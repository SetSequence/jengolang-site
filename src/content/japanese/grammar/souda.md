restrictions:
  - text: "Hearsay そうだ does not inflect: there is no ✗そうな／✗そうに and no past ✗降{ふ}るそうだった form. It only ends a sentence reporting what you heard. (The inflecting そう — おいしそうな — is the appearance そうだ.)"
  - text: "Attaches to the **plain form**. Attaching to the stem makes it the appearance そうだ instead: 降{ふ}るそうだ = 'I hear it'll rain'; 降{ふ}りそうだ = 'it looks about to rain.'"
notes:
  - text: "The source is often named with 〜によると／〜によれば ('according to ~') or 〜の話{はなし}では."
contrasts:
  - slug: "souda-2"
    label: "〜そうだ (appearance)"
    distinction: "Same surface だ, opposite job — and the attachment tells them apart: plain form + そうだ = hearsay (降{ふ}るそうだ); stem + そうだ = appearance (降{ふ}りそうだ)."
  - slug: "rashii"
    label: "らしい"
    distinction: "Both pass on secondhand information, but 伝聞{でんぶん}の そうだ reports it straight ('I hear ~'); らしい is hazier and lets the speaker stay non-committal ('apparently ~')."
  - slug: "youda"
    label: "ようだ"
    distinction: "そうだ (hearsay) just relays what someone told you; ようだ is the speaker's own inference from evidence. Different sources of knowing — report vs deduction."
