  en: "The teacher made the students read the book."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Godan"
    form: "う-row → あ-row + せる"
    example: "書{か}く → 書{か}かせる"
  - attaches_to: "Godan 〜う"
    form: "→ 〜わせる"
    example: "買{か}う → 買{か}わせる"
  - attaches_to: "Ichidan"
    form: "drop る + させる"
    example: "食{た}べる → 食{た}べさせる"
  - attaches_to: "Irregular"
    form: "する → させる、来{く}る → 来{こ}させる"
    example: "する → させる"
usageSetting: "Covers both coercion ('make') and permission ('let'); the nuance depends on context and on を vs に. The result conjugates as an ichidan verb (〜させて, 〜させない …)."
examples:
  - jp: "母{はは}は妹{いもうと}に部屋{へや}を掃除{そうじ}させた。"
    en: "Mother made my younger sister clean the room."
    note: "coercion"
  - jp: "子{こ}どもを公園{こうえん}で遊{あそ}ばせる。"
    en: "I let the kids play in the park."
    note: "permission"
  - jp: "すみません、少{すこ}し休{やす}ませてください。"
    en: "Excuse me, please let me rest a little."
    note: "〜させてください (ask permission)"
  - jp: "部長{ぶちょう}は部下{ぶか}に資料{しりょう}を作{つく}らせた。"
    en: "The manager had his subordinate prepare the documents."
  - jp: "親{おや}を心配{しんぱい}させたくない。"
    en: "I don't want to make my parents worry."
    note: "emotion causative"
restrictions:
  - text: "With a transitive verb the person made to act is marked に, not を (the を is taken by the object): 子{こ}どもに野菜{やさい}を食{た}べさせる, not ×子{こ}どもを野菜{やさい}を食{た}べさせる."
contrasts:
  - slug: "morau"
    label: "〜てもらう"
    distinction: "Causative させる = the subject directs or permits the action; 〜てもらう = the subject receives a favor someone else performs. Opposite direction of control."
notes:
  - text: "The causative-passive (〜させられる, 'be made to do') is built by adding the passive to this form."
