equivalents:
  - "I wonder if ~ / could it be that ~? — explanatory の + conjectural だろう + question か: a weighed, often introspective doubt"
nuance: "のだろうか layers three pieces: の frames the matter as the thing in question, だろう adds conjecture, か makes it a question turned over in the mind. The result is heavier and more reflective than かな — it suits writing and serious pondering ('I wonder whether this is really right'). The polite form is のでしょうか; casual speech often drops to んだろうか."
keySentence:
  jp: "本当{ほんとう}にこれでいいのだろうか。"
  en: "I wonder if this is really all right."

    form: "… plain + のだろうか / Nなのだろうか"
    example: "正{ただ}しいのだろうか / 必要{ひつよう}なのだろうか"
examples:

    en: "I wonder why he said something like that."
  - jp: "人類{じんるい}はどこへ向{む}かっているのだろうか。"
    en: "Where is humanity heading, one wonders?"
  - jp: "この選択{せんたく}は正{ただ}しかったのだろうか。"
    en: "Was this choice really the right one, I wonder."
contrasts:
  - slug: "kana"
    label: "〜かな"
    distinction: "Both wonder, but かな is light, casual musing; のだろうか is heavier and more introspective/written — a doubt you genuinely weigh, not a passing 'hmm'."
  - slug: "darou"
    label: "だろう"
    distinction: "Plain だろう conjectures ('probably ~'); adding のか turns the conjecture into a question you put to yourself ('I wonder whether ~'), with の framing the issue as the point in doubt."
notes:
  - text: "Without の — 〜だろうか — also means 'I wonder', but のだろうか specifically frames the doubt as being about the underlying situation/explanation, which is why it feels more deliberative."
