  - "even by ~ / even if it means ~ — naming an extreme method one is willing to resort to in order to achieve a goal"
nuance: "The て-form + でも presents a drastic, normally-unwelcome *means* and says the speaker would use even that to get what they want. 借金{しゃっきん}してでも、その車{くるま}を買{か}いたい ('I want that car even if it means going into debt'). The first clause is the extreme measure; the second is a strong desire, intention, or resolve. It answers 'how far would you go?' — and the answer is 'even this far'."

  - slug: "sae-ba"
