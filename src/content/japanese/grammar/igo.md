restrictions:
  - text: "以後 includes the point named and everything after it, and is formal. In casual speech 〜のあとで or 〜から is far more natural (三時{さんじ}のあとで, not 三時{さんじ}以後{いご}, when chatting)."
contrasts:
  - slug: "ikou"
    label: "以降{いこう}"
    distinction: "Nearly synonymous as 'from ~ onward,' but 以降{いこう} is purely a range marker tied to a stated time (四月{しがつ}以降{いこう}). 以後{いご} can also stand alone as 'henceforth' with a corrective tone (以後{いご}気{き}をつけます = 'I'll be careful from now on'), which 以降{いこう} cannot."
  - slug: "irai"
    label: "〜以来{いらい}"
    distinction: "以来{いらい} looks back from a past starting point up to the present ('ever since'), stressing the continuous stretch. 以後{いご} simply marks everything after a point, with no 'up to now' nuance."
  - slug: "ato-de"
    label: "〜あと(で)"
    distinction: "あとで is the everyday 'after ~ing' for ordering two events. 以後{いご} is a formal time-boundary ('from this point onward') rather than 'after doing something.'"
