contrasts:

    label: "〜ごろ"
    distinction: "ごろ marks an approximate point in *time only* (三時{さんじ}ごろ 'around 3:00'); あたり approximates time, place, or quantity, and for time feels slightly vaguer/softer."
  - slug: "gurai"
    label: "〜ぐらい"
    distinction: "ぐらい/くらい gives an approximate *amount or degree* (千円{せんえん}ぐらい 'about ¥1000'); あたり picks an approximate *point or vicinity* on a scale (この辺{あた}り 'around here')."
