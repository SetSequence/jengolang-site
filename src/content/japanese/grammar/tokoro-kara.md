It's the standard frame for explaining how a name or nickname arose — 富士山{ふじさん}に似{に}ているところから、「〇〇富士{ふじ}」と呼{よ}ばれる ('from the fact that it resembles Mt. Fuji, it's called the such-and-such Fuji') — and for tracing a conclusion back to its grounds. Belongs to written/explanatory Japanese."
