jlpt: "N3"

equivalents:
  - "about the same as ~ / as ~ as — states that two things are roughly equal in degree"
nuance: "と同{おな}じくらい marks an equal-degree comparison: A is 'as ~ as' B. 同{おな}じ ('same') fixes the equivalence and くらい ('about') makes it approximate, so it's the natural 'as…as' — the positive counterpart of 〜ほど〜ない ('not as…as'). The quality usually follows: 兄{あに}と同{おな}じくらい背{せ}が高{たか}い ('as tall as my brother')."
keySentence:
  jp: "弟{おとうと}は父{ちち}と同{おな}じくらい背{せ}が高{たか}い。"
  en: "My younger brother is about as tall as our father."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun + と同じくらい + adjective"
    form: "N + と同{おな}じくらい + Aい/Naだ"
    example: "去年{きょねん}と同{おな}じくらい / これと同{おな}じくらい"
examples:

    en: "This shop is about as cheap as the one by the station."
  - jp: "今日{きょう}は昨日{きのう}と同{おな}じくらい暑{あつ}い。"
    en: "Today is about as hot as yesterday."
  - jp: "彼女{かのじょ}は私{わたし}と同{おな}じくらいピアノが上手{じょうず}だ。"
    en: "She's about as good at piano as I am."
contrasts:
  - slug: "hodo-nai"
    label: "〜ほど〜ない"
    distinction: "Mirror images of degree: と同{おな}じくらい says two things are *equal* ('as ~ as'); 〜ほど〜ない says the subject *falls short* ('not as ~ as'). Equality vs deficit."
  - slug: "to-onaji-de"
    label: "〜と同じで"
    distinction: "と同{おな}じで says things are the *same in kind/identical*; と同{おな}じくらい says they're the *same in degree/amount* (roughly equal on a scale)."
notes:
  - text: "ぐらい freely replaces くらい here (父{ちち}と同{おな}じぐらい). Without 同{おな}じ, plain 〜くらい〜 can also express 'as ~ as' for extent."
