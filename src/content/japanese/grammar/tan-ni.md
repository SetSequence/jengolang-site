restrictions:
  - text: "単{たん}に modifies a verb or clause and usually wants a closing だけ or に過{す}ぎない to complete the 'merely … and no more' frame. Before a NOUN, the form is 単{たん}なる ('a mere ~'), not 単{たん}に."
contrasts:

  - slug: "ni-suginai"
    label: "に過ぎない"
    distinction: "Often combined: 単{たん}に is the adverb at the front; に過{す}ぎない is the predicate that closes 'is nothing more than ~'."
notes:
