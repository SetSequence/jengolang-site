  - "why don't you ~? / how about ~ing? — suggests a course of action to the listener using a たら conditional left hanging"
nuance: "〜たらどう is literally 'if you did ~, how (would it be)?' with the second half trailed off. It proposes the action as a solution to the listener's situation, so it works as advice — but precisely because it tells the other person what to do, it can sound pushy or even nagging if the listener didn't ask. Gentler with friends, riskier upward."
