    label: "こと (nominalizer)"
    distinction: "Both nominalize a clause. の fits concrete, perceived, here-and-now events (歌{うた}うのが聞{き}こえる — hear someone sing); こと fits abstract facts, rules, and reported content (歌{うた}うことが好{す}き). Perception verbs (見{み}る, 聞{き}く) take only の."
notes:
