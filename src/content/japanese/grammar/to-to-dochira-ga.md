equivalents:
  - "between ~ and ~, which is more ~? — the set question for comparing exactly two items"
nuance: "This is the fixed frame for asking a two-way comparison: name both items with と, then ask どちら(が) ('which of the two') + the quality. The answer comes back with 〜のほうが. どちら is the neutral/polite 'which'; casual speech uses どっち. For three or more items you switch to どれ / 一番{いちばん} instead — どちら is strictly two."

  en: "Coffee or tea — which would you prefer?"
formation:

    form: "N と N と、どちらが + adjective(ですか)"
    example: "夏{なつ}と冬{ふゆ}と、どちらが…"
examples:

    en: "Which is faster, the train or the bus?"

    en: "Which do you like, meat or fish?"

contrasts:

    label: "〜の中で〜が一番"
    distinction: "どちらが compares exactly *two* options; for three or more, set the group with の中{なか}で and ask 一番{いちばん} ('which is the most…')."
  - slug: "no-hou-ga"
    label: "のほうが"
    distinction: "This is the *question*; the *answer* uses のほうが — Aと Bと、どちらが…？ → Bのほうが…. They're two halves of one comparison."
notes:
