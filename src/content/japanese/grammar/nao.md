nuance: "なお heads a sentence that *adds a side note or caveat* once the main statement is finished — the staple of notices, announcements, and formal writing. 説明会{せつめいかい}は二時{にじ}からです。なお、筆記用具{ひっきようぐ}をご持参{じさん}ください ('The session starts at two. Note: please bring writing materials'). Unlike さらに/その上, which pile on more supporting points, なお appends auxiliary information the reader needs — a condition, exception, or housekeeping detail — set slightly apart from the core message."
