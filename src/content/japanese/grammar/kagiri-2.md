nuance: "A different 限り from the 'as long as / as far as' conditional. Here 限{かぎ}り means a fixed *limit*. Attached straight to a noun it means 'only / just this one ~': 今回{こんかい}限{かぎ}り ('just this once'), 一日{いちにち}限{かぎ}り ('one day only'). With を — 〜を限{かぎ}りに — it marks a thing as the cut-off point, 'as of ~ / ending with ~': 今日{きょう}を限{かぎ}りに ('as of today, no more'). The idiom 声{こえ}を限{かぎ}りに ('at the top of one's voice') uses the same 'to the very limit' image."
