contrasts:
  - slug: "wa"
    label: "は (plain topic order)"
    distinction: "君{きみ}に会{あ}いたい just states the wish. 会{あ}いたいのは君{きみ}だ restructures it to single out 君{きみ} as the one and only focus — the cleft answers an implicit 'which/who/where exactly?'"
  - slug: "no-da"
    label: "のだ"
    distinction: "Both use の, but のだ frames a whole statement as an explanation (行{い}くんだ); のは〜だ splits one sentence to highlight a specific part (行{い}くのは私{わたし}だ — the one going is me)."
