keigo: "none"
family: "connective"
freq: "common"
jlpt: "N3"
confidence: "high"
equivalents:
  - "the reason is that ~ / this is because ~ — a formal sentence-opener introducing the reason for the previous statement"
nuance: "なぜなら starts a *new sentence* that explains the one before it, and it typically closes with からだ / ためだ: 行{い}けない。なぜなら、仕事{しごと}があるからだ ('I can't go. The reason is I have work'). It's explicit and somewhat formal — common in essays, presentations, and logical argument. から/ので embed the reason inside one sentence; なぜなら sets it out as a separate, signposted clause. なぜならば is the fuller, stiffer variant."
keySentence:
  jp: "私{わたし}は反対{はんたい}だ。なぜなら、危険{きけん}すぎるからだ。"
  en: "I'm against it — the reason being that it's too dangerous."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Sentence-initial; the explaining clause usually ends in からだ／ためだ"
    form: "(statement)。なぜなら、〜からだ。"
    example: "なぜなら、〜からだ / なぜならば、〜ためである"
examples:
  - jp: "計画{けいかく}は延期{えんき}された。なぜなら予算{よさん}が足{た}りなかったからだ。"
    en: "The plan was postponed, because the budget fell short."
  - jp: "運動{うんどう}は大切{たいせつ}だ。なぜなら、健康{けんこう}を保{たも}つからである。"
    en: "Exercise is important, because it keeps you healthy."
  - jp: "彼{かれ}を信頼{しんらい}している。なぜなら、一度{いちど}も嘘{うそ}をついたことがないからだ。"
    en: "I trust him, because he has never once lied."
contrasts:
  - slug: "kara"
    label: "〜から"
    distinction: "から packs the reason into the same sentence ('because X, Y'); なぜなら opens a separate sentence to spell the reason out, more explicit and formal, and pairs with a closing からだ."
  - slug: "dakara"
    label: "だから"
    distinction: "Opposite order of logic: だから introduces the *result* ('so, therefore ~'); なぜなら introduces the *reason* ('this is because ~'). Result-first vs reason-first."
notes:
  - text: "The closing からだ/ためだ is expected — leaving it off (なぜなら、危険{きけん}だ) sounds incomplete in careful writing. なぜかというと is the more conversational equivalent."
