contrasts:

  - slug: "rareru"
    label: "honorific/spontaneous られる"
    distinction: "The identical shape られる is also honorific and potential. 食{た}べられる can mean 'is eaten' (passive), 'can eat' (potential), or '(an honored person) eats' — context decides."
  - slug: "saseru"
    label: "させる (causative)"
    distinction: "させる = make/let someone act; られる = the subject is acted upon. The two combine into the causative-passive させられる ('be made to do')."
