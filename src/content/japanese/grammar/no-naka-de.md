jlpt: "N4"

equivalents:
  - "among ~ / of all ~ / within ~ — sets the group or range from which something is singled out"
nuance: "の中{なか}で marks the set you're choosing or comparing within — 'out of all these'. It's the standard setup for a superlative or a pick: クラスの中{なか}で… ('of everyone in the class…'). For a category rather than a listed group, 〜の中{なか}でも ('even among ~') stresses standing out. The literal 'inside' meaning (箱{はこ}の中{なか}で, 'inside the box') is the same word used spatially."
keySentence:
  jp: "クラスの中{なか}で、彼{かれ}が一番{いちばん}背{せ}が高{たか}い。"
  en: "Of everyone in the class, he is the tallest."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun (a group/category) + の中で"
    form: "N + の中で"
    example: "三人{さんにん}の中{なか}で / 季節{きせつ}の中{なか}で"
examples:
  - jp: "果物{くだもの}の中{なか}で、何{なに}が一番{いちばん}好{す}きですか。"
    en: "Of all fruits, which do you like best?"
  - jp: "この三{みっ}つの中{なか}から選{えら}んでください。"
    en: "Please choose from among these three."
  - jp: "日本{にほん}の都市{とし}の中{なか}でも、京都{きょうと}は特別{とくべつ}だ。"
    en: "Even among Japan's cities, Kyoto is special."
contrasts:
  - slug: "no-naka-de-ga-ichiban"
    label: "〜の中で〜が一番"
    distinction: "の中{なか}で alone sets the group; add 〜が一番{いちばん} to complete the superlative and name the top member of that group."
  - slug: "de"
    label: "で (scope)"
    distinction: "With a noun naming an inherent range, plain で can replace の中{なか}で: 世界{せかい}で一番{いちばん} ('the best in the world') — no 中{なか} needed when the noun is already the whole scope."
notes:
  - text: "Use から (の中{なか}から) when *choosing/taking* out of the set: 三{みっ}つの中{なか}から選{えら}ぶ ('pick from three')."
