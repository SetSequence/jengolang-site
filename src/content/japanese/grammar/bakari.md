contrasts:
  - slug: "dake"
    label: "〜だけ"
    distinction: "だけ states a precise limit and is neutral (三人{さんにん}だけ = 'exactly three'). ばかり means 'nothing but ~' and colours the situation as excessive or one-sided (お菓子{かし}ばかり = 'sweets and nothing else')."
  - slug: "shika"
    label: "しか"
    distinction: "しか takes a negative and means 'only, and that's not enough' (水{みず}しかない). ばかり takes a positive predicate and emphasises that one thing dominates everything else."
  - slug: "ta-bakari"
    label: "〜たばかり"
    distinction: "Attached to the た-form, ばかり instead means 'just (now) did ~' (着{つ}いたばかり = 'just arrived') — a time sense, not the 'only/nothing but' sense here."
