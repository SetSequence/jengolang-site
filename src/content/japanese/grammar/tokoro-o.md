  - "at a moment/situation when ~ — marks the scene as the thing acted upon: either a polite 'when you're ~' apology, or 'caught right as one was ~ing'"
nuance: "ところ names a moment or situation, and を makes that scene the *object* of the next verb. Two everyday uses. (1) A set politeness frame acknowledging the listener's situation before imposing on it: お忙{いそが}しいところを、すみません ('sorry to trouble you when you're busy'). (2) Catching/interrupting someone mid-action: 逃{に}げようとするところを捕{つか}まえた ('caught him just as he was about to flee'). The shared idea is that the following verb lands *on* that moment."
