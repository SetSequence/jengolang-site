  - "~, and ~ (and what's more) — explicitly stacks two or more reasons or qualities, all pointing toward a conclusion"
nuance: "Repeating し spells out the cumulative listing of single し: you give several grounds side by side, building a case. Very common before a decision or evaluation — 安{やす}いし、近{ちか}いし、便利{べんり}だ ('it's cheap, it's close, it's convenient'). The implied 'so…' conclusion often follows or is left understood."

contrasts:

    label: "し"

    label: "〜も〜も"
    distinction: "〜も〜も lists parallel *items* with one predicate ('both A and B are…'); 〜し〜し lists parallel *clauses/reasons* ('it's A, and it's B, …')."
