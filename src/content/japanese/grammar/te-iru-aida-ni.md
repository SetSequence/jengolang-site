nuance: "This is 〜間{あいだ}に with the span expressed as a ている progressive: 'while X is going on, Y happens (once)'. The に means Y occurs at *some point* inside X, not for its whole duration. The two clauses often have different subjects (寝{ね}ている間{あいだ}に誰{だれ}か来{き}た). Drop the に and Y instead spans all of X."
