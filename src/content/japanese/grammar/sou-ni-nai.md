  - text: "This is the negative of the appearance-based 〜そうだ ('looks like'), so it attaches to the ます-stem of a verb, not to the plain form. It judges from observation that something won't happen — it isn't a flat statement that it won't (that's 〜ない)."
contrasts:
  - slug: "souda-2"
    label: "〜そうだ"

  - slug: "sou-ni-naru"
    label: "そうになる"
    distinction: "Same appearance そう base, but そうになる means 'almost happened' (a near-miss: 転{ころ}びそうになった = 'almost fell'), while そうにない means 'unlikely to happen at all.'"
