nuance: "逆{ぎゃく} ('reverse, opposite') + に: と逆{ぎゃく}に says the situation that follows is the *reverse* of the named one. 予想{よそう}と逆{ぎゃく}に、株価{かぶか}は急騰{きゅうとう}した ('contrary to expectations, stock prices shot up'). It's stronger than 'merely different' — it points to the polar opposite. Often used with 予想{よそう}・予測{よそく}・期待{きたい} to flag an outcome that went the other way."

contrasts:

    label: "〜反面"
    distinction: "反面{はんめん} presents two contrasting sides of the *same* thing ('convenient, but on the other hand expensive'); と逆{ぎゃく}に sets one thing as the *polar opposite* of a separate reference ('contrary to X, Y')."
  - slug: "ni-taishite"
    label: "に対して"
    distinction: "に対して in its contrastive sense lines two things up to compare ('A is big, whereas B is small'); と逆{ぎゃく}に specifically says the second is the *reverse* of the first, not merely different."
notes:
