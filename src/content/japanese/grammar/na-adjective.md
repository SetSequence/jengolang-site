family: "adjective"
prereqs: []
related: ["i-adjective", "de-3", "ni-naru", "da"]

noindex: false
equivalents:
  - "descriptive words that behave like nouns — they borrow the copula (静{しず}か + です / だ) to make a sentence and add な before a noun"
nuance: "Unlike an い-adjective, a な-adjective has no conjugation of its own. It sits still and lets the copula do the work — です / だ for the present, でした / だった for the past — exactly as a noun would. The name comes from the な it needs before a noun."
keySentence:
  jp: "この部屋{へや}は静{しず}かです。"
  en: "This room is quiet."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Present (polite)"
    form: "stem + です"
    example: "静{しず}かです"
  - attaches_to: "Present (plain)"
    form: "stem + だ"
    example: "静{しず}かだ"
  - attaches_to: "Negative"
    form: "stem + じゃないです / ではありません"
    example: "静{しず}かじゃないです"
  - attaches_to: "Past"
    form: "stem + でした / だった"
    example: "静{しず}かでした"
  - attaches_to: "Past negative"
    form: "stem + じゃなかったです"
    example: "静{しず}かじゃなかったです"
  - attaches_to: "Before a noun"
    form: "stem + な + noun"
    example: "静{しず}かな部屋{へや}"
  - attaches_to: "Adverb"
    form: "stem + に"
    example: "静{しず}かに (quietly)"
usageSetting: "な-adjectives borrow the copula for tense and negation, so the endings are just です / だ・じゃない・でした. The plain だ-forms (静{しず}かだ, 静{しず}かだった) are the casual register introduced in the next unit; the polite です-forms above are enough to describe things now."
restrictions:
  - text: "Never conjugate a な-adjective like an い-adjective: 'was quiet' is 静{しず}かだった / 静{しず}かでした, never ×静{しず}かかった. The traps 綺麗{きれい} and 嫌{きら}い end in い but are な-adjectives — 綺麗{きれい}な花{はな}, 綺麗{きれい}でした."
  - text: "Before a noun you must insert な (静{しず}かな町{まち}); dropping it (×静{しず}か町{まち}) is ungrammatical. This な is what names the class."
contrasts:
  - slug: "i-adjective"
    label: "い形容詞"
    distinction: "い-adjectives change their own ending (高{たか}い → 高{たか}かった). な-adjectives keep one fixed shape, let the copula carry tense and negation, and need な before a noun."
  - slug: "de-3"
    label: "〜で (な-adj)"
    distinction: "To link a な-adjective to another clause ('quiet and clean'), the copula's て-form で does it: 静{しず}かで、きれいだ. That connecting で is its own point."
  - slug: "ni-naru"
    label: "〜になる"
    distinction: "To say something becomes quiet, a な-adjective takes に + なる (静{しず}かになる) — parallel to an い-adjective's 〜くなる (高{たか}くなる)."

    note: "negative じゃない + です"
  - jp: "子{こ}どもの頃{ころ}、この公園{こうえん}は有名{ゆうめい}でした。"
    en: "When I was a child, this park was famous."
    note: "past でした"
  - jp: "彼女{かのじょ}はいつも元気{げんき}な人{ひと}です。"
    en: "She is always an energetic person."
    note: "な before a noun"
  - jp: "図書館{としょかん}では静{しず}かに歩{ある}く。"
    en: "At the library, walk quietly."
    note: "adverb に modifying a verb"
notes:
  - text: "A quick test: form the past. い-adjectives take 〜かった (高{たか}かった); な-adjectives take だった / でした (静{しず}かだった). This correctly sorts the traps 綺麗{きれい} and 嫌{きら}い, which end in い but are な-adjectives (綺麗{きれい}だった, 嫌{きら}いでした)."
