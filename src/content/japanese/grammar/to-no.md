  - "the ~ that / ~ to the effect that — attaches quoted or reported content directly to a noun, formally"
nuance: "との is the compressed, written-register equivalent of という when it modifies a noun of communication or thought — 連絡{れんらく}・知{し}らせ・指示{しじ}・報告{ほうこく}・意見{いけん}・考{かんが}え. 「会議{かいぎ}は延期{えんき}」との連絡{れんらく}があった ('there was word to the effect that the meeting is postponed'). It packages what was said/decided/thought and hangs it on the following noun. Common in news, business writing, and formal speech; という is the everyday spoken counterpart."

contrasts:
  - slug: "to-iu"
    label: "という"
    distinction: "Same job — attaching reported content to a noun — but という is the neutral/spoken default (彼{かれ}が来{く}るという話{はなし}); との is its formal, written contraction, favoured with report/communication nouns in news and business."
  - slug: "to-no-koto"
    label: "とのこと"
    distinction: "とのこと is との + こと used to *end a sentence* as relayed hearsay ('I'm told that ~'); この との modifies a following noun ('the report that ~')."
