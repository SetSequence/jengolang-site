title: "なんか — things like / (dismissively) ~ of all things"
canonical: "なんか"
reading: "なんか"
register: ["casual-spoken"]
keigo: "none"
family: "particle"
freq: "common"
jlpt: "N3"
confidence: "high"
senses:
  - label: "things like / such as"
    equivalents:
      - "things like ~ / ~ or something — casually cites an example, the colloquial form of など"
    keySentence:
      jp: "週末{しゅうまつ}は映画{えいが}なんかどう？"
      en: "How about a movie or something this weekend?"
    examples:
      - jp: "お茶{ちゃ}なんか飲{の}みませんか。"
        en: "Would you like some tea or something?"
      - jp: "甘{あま}いものなんかが食{た}べたい。"
        en: "I feel like eating something sweet."
  - label: "belittling / dismissive"
    equivalents:
      - "(the likes of) ~ / ~ of all things — downplays or rejects the marked item; humble on oneself, scornful on others"
    keySentence:
      jp: "お金{かね}なんかいらない。"
      en: "I don't need money or anything like it."
    examples:
      - jp: "私{わたし}なんかにできるでしょうか。"
        en: "Could someone like me really do it?"
      - jp: "宿題{しゅくだい}なんかしたくない。"
        en: "I don't want to do homework (of all things)."
nuance: "なんか is the casual, spoken contraction of など. It cites a loose example ('~ or something') and, attached to a thing you dismiss or downplay, carries a belittling tone — humble on yourself (私{わたし}なんか), scornful or impatient on others (勉強{べんきょう}なんか). As a filler, なんか also peppers casual speech ('like, kinda'). Conversational only — など is the neutral/written equivalent."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Nouns (replacing/after a particle); also as a discourse filler"
    form: "N + なんか"
    example: "ドラマなんか / 君{きみ}なんか"
contrasts:
  - slug: "nado"
    label: "など"
    distinction: "Same two jobs (example-citing + belittling); なんか is casual-spoken, など is neutral-to-formal and the written choice."
  - slug: "nante"
    label: "なんて"
    distinction: "Both belittle/cite casually, but なんて can also quote a phrase or clause (帰{かえ}るなんて, 'saying you'll leave'); なんか attaches to plain nouns."
notes:
  - text: "Don't confuse with 何{なに}か ('something', the indefinite) — written the same in kana but stressed and used differently; なんか here is the など-derived particle."
