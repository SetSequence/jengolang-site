  jp: "兄{あに}は学生{がくせい}で、22歳{さい}です。"
  en: "My older brother is a student and is 22 years old."
formation:
  - attaches_to: "Noun"

  - attaches_to: "na-adjective"
    form: "na-adj + で"
    example: "静{しず}か → 静{しず}かで"
usageSetting: "Replaces だ/です between clauses; the final clause carries the real tense and politeness (で … です / で … だった)."
examples:
  - jp: "ここは静{しず}かで便利{べんり}な町{まち}だ。"
    en: "This is a quiet and convenient town."
  - jp: "彼女{かのじょ}は医者{いしゃ}で、母親{ははおや}でもある。"
    en: "She is a doctor and also a mother."
  - jp: "今日{きょう}は休{やす}みで、どこにも行{い}かない。"
    en: "Today is a day off, and I'm not going anywhere."
contrasts:
  - slug: "kute"
    label: "〜くて"
    distinction: "Use で to link a noun or na-adjective (学生{がくせい}で, 静{しず}かで). Use くて for the same link with an i-adjective (広{ひろ}くて)."
  - slug: "de"
    label: "で (particle)"
    distinction: "Same kana, different word. This で is the copula te-form ('is ~ and'). The particle で marks place of action, means, or cause — 図書館{としょかん}で勉強{べんきょう}する (study at the library)."
notes:
  - text: "This で is how だ/です joins clauses, just as a verb's te-form does — it is the noun/na-adjective counterpart of 〜て and 〜くて."
