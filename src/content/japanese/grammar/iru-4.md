  - text: "要{い}る is a u-verb despite ending in -る: the negative is 要{い}らない (not 要{い}ない) and the polite form is 要{い}ります. Treating it as a ru-verb (要{い}ない) is a common mistake."
  - text: "What you need is marked with が, not を — like 好{す}きだ and できる, 要{い}る takes its target with が (お金{かね}が要{い}る, never お金{かね}を要{い}る)."
contrasts:
  - slug: "hitsuyou-ga-aru"
    label: "必要がある"
    distinction: "要{い}る is everyday and applies to concrete things you need (money, a visa, a tool). 必要{ひつよう}だ / 必要{ひつよう}がある is more formal and covers abstract needs and actions too (確認{かくにん}する必要{ひつよう}がある = 'there's a need to confirm'). You can't say 〜する要{い}る for an action."
