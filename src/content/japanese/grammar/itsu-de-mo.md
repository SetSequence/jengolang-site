      - jp: "週末{しゅうまつ}はいつも家{いえ}にいる。"
        en: "I'm always home on weekends."
nuance: "The で is the difference. いつでも (with で) is the question-word-+-でも free-choice indefinite — 'any time you choose'. いつも (without で) is the plain frequency adverb 'always/usually', describing what habitually happens. Don't swap them: いつも来{く}る = 'always comes (habit)'; いつでも来{こ}られる = 'can come any time'."
