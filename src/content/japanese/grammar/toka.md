nuance: "とか (from と + か) is the spoken, vaguer cousin of や. It picks out examples without committing to a full list, and unlike や it can quote actions and whole phrases too: 「行{い}く」とか言{い}ってた ('said something like \"I'll go\"'). With a single item it softens and hedges — コーヒーとか飲{の}む？ ('wanna grab coffee or something?') is gentler than naming it outright. Casual; overuse sounds noncommittal."
