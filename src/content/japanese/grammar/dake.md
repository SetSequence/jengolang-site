restrictions:
  - text: "だけ takes whatever predicate fits, positive or negative (千円{せんえん}だけある = 'there's just 1,000 yen'). If you want the 'and that's not enough' nuance, you need しか + a negative instead — だけ never carries that feeling on its own."
contrasts:
  - slug: "shika"
    label: "しか"
    distinction: "だけ is a plain limit with a positive verb (千円{せんえん}だけある = 'there's exactly 1,000 yen'). しか always takes a negative and adds a 'that's all, less than I'd like' nuance (千円{せんえん}しかない = 'I've only got 1,000 yen')."
  - slug: "bakari"
    label: "〜ばかり"
    distinction: "だけ states a precise limit; ばかり means 'nothing but ~' with a sense of excess or one-sidedness (テレビばかり見{み}ている = 'does nothing but watch TV')."
